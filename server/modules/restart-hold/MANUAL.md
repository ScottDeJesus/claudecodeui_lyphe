## MAN-9553 — restart-hold

The server's one picture of "busy", for the dev supervisor's restart hold: a builder's save under `server/` waits for this server to say it is quiet before it boots a successor and retires this one, so a restart never lands in the middle of a sentence of his or hers. His own **Restart server** press, a reboot request, `systemctl restart` and a deploy are never held.

**Busy while any of these holds:** a Kaylee turn is in flight on either account, her own last turn included; a chat run is streaming (the one reason that *yields*: the supervisor stops counting it once a save has been held 30 minutes — chat runs survive a handover on their keepalive hosts); a press of his is open on her panel (the talk button recording, her name opening a recording, or a clip out for transcription) or her voice is still sounding there; less than the quiet tail (90 s) has passed since anything was said or typed to her; or this boot is a handover successor that has not finished its takeover duties (re-adopting the chat runs, saying Kaylee's unanswered words again) — its picture is empty by omission until then, and a save asked about at that instant would restart it straight after its takeover. The ears merely *listening* for her name are not a press — with the switch on that mic is open all day and counting it would hold every save for ever.

**Events only.** `restartHold` (`restart-hold.service.ts`) is told by its sources: `kayleeTurn` and `spokenToKaylee` by the companion session (`companion-session.ts` → `companion-account.ts`), `chatRuns` by `followChatRuns` off the run registry's change feed (`server/index.ts`), `takingOver` by the entrypoint around a handover boot's takeover duties, `panelSounding` and `panelGone` by the chat socket (`companion.sounding { press, voice }`, sent on change only by `src/modules/companion/hooks/useCompanionSounding.ts`; a closed socket takes its state with it). The tail is one timer, armed by his last word and re-armed by the next; the only other clock is the 3 s settle before `quiet` is told, so work that hands on to more work (a turn's end, then her context reading and her last turn) is never read as a quiet instant. Busy is told at once.

**Carried over the existing IPC channel** by `reportHold` in `server/supervised-boot.ts`: `{ type: 'busy', reasons: [{ why, yields }] }` or `{ type: 'quiet' }`, once right after `ready` with the current picture and then on every change. The supervisor's half (`supervisor.mjs`, `hold.mjs`) — the ceiling, a boot carrying the saves settled before its spawn, a save's successor stopped when the server went busy again while it booted, and the journal lines — is in `deploy/dev-supervisor/README.md`. `GET /api/restart-hold` (authenticated) answers `{ busy, reasons }` for a person or a script that wants to pick a quiet moment, such as a restart of the whole unit.

**Measured, not guessed.** The 90 s tail covers 85.4% of 1,035 returns to an idle her inside a conversation (`.verify/restart-hold-tail.mjs`; half come within 9 s). The 30-minute ceiling: over one day's journal chat runs alone would have held 11 of 23 saves past it, the longest 128 minutes (`.verify/restart-hold-waits.mjs`; the replay counts any transcript writer as a chat run, so it is an upper bound, and it moves with the data — 12 of 28 a day later). The live run that holds a save behind a real conversation on account 1 is `.verify/restart-hold-live.mjs`.
