## INV-10856 — server/modules/companion/session — a restart mid-turn: the retiring server lets her turn finish, the successor holds her until the takeover, and the bounds that cover it

A restart that must happen mid-turn does not cut her off: the retiring server lets her turn finish, and the successor holds what is said meanwhile. Edits that break it:

- THE EXIT WAITS FOR HER, NOTHING ELSE DOES. `shutdownRuntimeServices` (`server/index.ts`) starts `companion.stop(COMPANION_TURN_BOUND_MS)` FIRST (the 503 for a message is in force before anything else stops) and awaits it LAST, beside the whole of the rest of the shutdown (cerberus, the HTTP drain, plugins). `stopAll` → `session.finishTurn(boundMs)` (`session/companion-restart.ts`) waits for the turn in flight to end by itself, at most 60 s, as an event (the turn tracker's end) and one timer (a second stop signal joins the same wait); then `speechCache.settled(sentenceBoundMs)` lets the sentences still streaming finish, at most the HTTP drain's own 4 s (`HTTP_DRAIN_BOUND_MS`); only then do `speechCache.end()`, `interrupt('server')` and `close('the server is stopping')` run, as they always did, and `stopMouth()` after every account. A turn that ends in the bound is answered (its words leave the ledger, the successor re-sends nothing); one that does not is stopped and its words are said again by the successor, unchanged. HER LAST TURN (her own compaction) is not waited for (`finishTurn` returns at once) and a turn that ends during the drain never begins it (`drain.draining` gates `closing.afterTurn`): a restart in it falls back to the house summarizer, as designed.
- THE PAGE KEEPS HEARING HER. The page's socket stays on the retiring server until it exits (`http-drain.ts`: websockets close at the exit), so her sentences and the turn's end arrive over it. The retiring server has closed its listener, so the page's speech pulls (`GET /speech/:turnId/:index`) are answered by the SUCCESSOR, which rebuilds the sentence from its log row (the row is in the shared database; the 5-minute window applies, the cache is not shared). A sentence already streaming from the retiring server is not cut: `speechCache.end()` waits for the turn and then for the sentence still streaming (a short turn ends while its first sentence is still on the wire). Measured 2026-11-04 (`.verify/kaylee-restart-turn.mjs`): sentence 0 streamed from the retiring pid, sentences 1 and 2 were pulled and served by the successor while the retiring pid was still draining. STILL CUT: what the successor emits during the drain (`companion_mouth` frames, the log line of what he says, the floor) goes to the successor's sockets, and the page has none there until the retiring server exits and the page reconnects (3 s, measured); her mouth follows her voice level meanwhile, and his own line shows on the panel at the reconnect. A PAGE THAT OPENS (or whose socket moves: in `after3` the first page's socket reconnected to the successor three times in the seconds after a second tab began to load, and `after2`, with no second tab, did not) ON THE SUCCESSOR DURING THE DRAIN has the same cut the other way round: the retiring server's frames never reach it, and until the takeover (up to ~70 s) its panel shows her idle: the successor's own session is empty, and the page's picture is that plus the shared log (not measured during the drain: the probe opens that panel only after everything settled, because opening it takes the floor). The takeover closes it: `companionService.resendUnanswered` first sends `{ kind: 'companion_resync' }` to every open socket (`companion-resync.ts`, the fan-out of `companion_memory`), and the page reads her picture again on it (`CompanionContext`). Measured live 2026-11-04 (`.verify/out/kaylee-restart-turn.after3.log`, a 43 s turn): the late tab drew mid-drain, read the picture again at the takeover and held all four sentences she said.
- THE SUCCESSOR HOLDS HER UNTIL THE TAKEOVER (`createTakeoverHold`). A session made on a handover boot (`createCompanionModule({ awaitingTakeover: handover })`) launches NOTHING until `resendUnanswered()`, the takeover: a second CLI resuming the conversation beside the first would fork the transcript. His words are kept on disk at once (`ledger.keep`) and said by the takeover's resend in order, each once; an alert or the news of a chat waits and goes after them; `warm` waits. The takeover is `companionService.resendUnanswered()`, which now asks EVERY account made meanwhile, not only those with words on the row, because it is what lifts the hold. Clear on a held session drops what was parked but leaves the hold standing: a push after it still waits for the takeover. If no takeover ever comes the module lets the hold go at `TAKEOVER_HOLD_MAX_MS` (110 s). Measured live 2026-11-04: a line typed 4.2 s after the press was kept, the successor launched her CLI only after the takeover (13.5 s) and answered it. The finished turn, measured live (`.verify/out/kaylee-restart-turn.after2.log`): a short answer after a slow read, a Restart-server press at T+0, the old server logged `her turn finished before her process closed` at T+8.1 s and exited `(0)` at T+8.6 s, the three sentences' audio arrived whole (each stream's bytes equal to the voice module's own count), nothing of the ask was re-sent and the line typed mid-restart was answered by the successor. A SENTENCE THE PAGE WAS NOT THERE FOR IS STILL SPOKEN: the successor's answer to that line ('Pineapple.') can come inside the page's 3 s socket-reconnect window, and the sentences of a turn the page followed can arrive with the re-seed after a socket moved. The pull (`utils/speechPull.ts`) speaks every turn the re-seeded picture holds sentences of that the page held no row of before, and the rest of a turn it moved on from before it was finished, in the order she said them (a turn older than the one being heard goes ahead of it while nothing of that one has been asked for), skips one whose newest sentence is over 60 s old (a page that slept), and asks for no sentence twice. Measured live (`after3`): the line typed 3.9 s after the press was answered by the successor at the takeover and its audio pulled whole (61440 B, the bytes the voice module synthesized) beside the three sentences of the first answer, each pulled once. That run also showed the order wrong (the answer to his line was pulled BEFORE the first answer's three sentences: the pull had moved onto the new turn before the re-read brought the older one); the pull now puts an owed turn older than the heard one ahead of it while nothing of that one has been asked for, replayed through the real engine on the run's recorded picture sequence (the restart itself was not repeated).
- THE BOUNDS COVER ONE ANOTHER, in this order, and change together: `COMPANION_TURN_BOUND_MS` 60 s < `COMPANION_STOP_BOUND_MS` 70 s (the turn, the 4 s for the sentence still streaming, her CLI's 5 s grace, a second; `server/index.ts`) < `STOP_TIMEOUT_MS` 75 s (the supervisor's SIGKILL of a SERVING child, `deploy/dev-supervisor/child.mjs`; a child that never became ready keeps `BOOT_STOP_TIMEOUT_MS`, 10 s) < `RUNS_READOPTED_WAIT_MS` 76 s (how long the successor holds `GET /api/providers/sessions/running` and the socket's host snapshot for a takeover that can now come ~70 s after READY; the page's 30 s request ceiling ends its read of it first and the five-second poll asks again, ONE READ AT A TIME (`SessionProtectionContext`): without that, a held read is added every tick, six fill the browser's six HTTP/1.1 connections and every other request of the tab waits behind them — measured 2026-11-04 on a stand-in that held the route, `GET /api/companion` took 4.5-5 s with six held and 7-30 ms with one. A tab opened in the hold draws no running chats until the takeover, the same list a tab shows before its first read) < systemd's `TimeoutStopUSec` 90 s (the supervisor's own shutdown stops its children together, so the unit stop costs the longest child, not the sum) < `TAKEOVER_HOLD_MAX_MS` 110 s. THE SUPERVISOR READS `child.mjs` ONCE, AT UNIT START: the 75 s bound is live only after `sudo systemctl restart cloudcli-server-dev`; until then the old 10 s SIGKILL cuts a drain that takes longer, and the successor says his words again (measured 2026-11-04: a digest of a long chat took 25 s; the retiring pid was killed at 10 s).
- THE COST OF THE WAIT: the successor's takeover (the chat re-adoption, the scheduled-message dispatcher, plugins, the update reconciler, Center's duties) is sent only when the retiring process has exited, so while a turn is finishing those duties wait up to 70 s. Nothing else is held back; with no turn in flight the exit takes ~1-2 s, as before.

Proof: `.verify/kaylee-restart-turn.mjs <label> [--interject "words"] [--prompt "words"] [--late-tab]` (`--late-tab` opens a second page the moment the successor is READY and, after everything settled, looks for every sentence of hers in its panel; one real run on account 1 on Haiku through the real panel, a forced restart; refuses unless account 2 has been quiet 6 minutes) and `.verify/companion-restart-turn.mjs` (the seams over a stand-in launcher and a scratch database: the finish, the bound, her last turn, the hold, the two ledgers; no model call).
