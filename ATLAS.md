cloudcli:server/modules/cerberus [store] Cerberus: fleet health. Wardens on other machines and three checks of center's own report results; center stores the ones that say something, works every status out when the screen reads, and serves it at `/api/cerberus`. It holds the catalog of fixes a person has written for a check, asks a warden to run one on a press or by its own auto-run rule, and keeps every run and its output. A person can ask for a read-only diagnosis of a red check: a Claude agent on center with six tools reads logs, files and status on the check's node and Cerberus history across the fleet, ends with a summary, its evidence and an optional proposed fix, and that proposal can be run once or promoted into the catalog; every diagnosis and step is kept. The warden gateway is a tailnet listener of its own (`CERBERUS_LISTEN`). Which row documents which subject: MAN-7816. (INV 38)
