# Stream

The companion's words as they arrive: each word lands faint and thin and settles to full, while a small working loop rides at the end.

- **Provide** the text so far as `text` and `live` while more is coming. `size`: body (default, `field-text`) or `large` (`transcript`, for what is also being spoken).
- Words present when it mounts are simply there; only words that arrive afterwards animate, once.
- Never stream your words — they are YourWords, whole. Put a Footprint above a Stream the companion wrote (Exchange does).
- `ariaLive` is off by default: a live region announcing every word is noise. Announce the finished answer instead.
