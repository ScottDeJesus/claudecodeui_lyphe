# Rule

A heading with its hairline, drawn out of the words when it arrives.

- **Provide** the heading as `children` (uppercase `telemetry-rule`); `align` `start` (the line runs right) or `end` (it runs left, toward the centre); `level` for its heading level (3).
- The hairline draws in on `ease-lock` each time the rule mounts — it arrives with its section.
- Use it to head a Sheet or a group of readouts inside an Opening. Never as a divider with no words.
