# Exchange

One turn between you and the companion: your words, whole, then its answer as it arrives, under its Footprint.

- **Provide** `said` (your line, exactly) with `via` and `at`, then `reply` (the answer so far) and `live` while it is still answering. `by` names the companion; `size="large"` when the answer is also spoken.
- The Footprint reads "Answering" while live and "Answered" after. Your words never get a Footprint, and the companion's never get `said`.
- A conversation is a column of Exchanges, newest last, in the center — only while you are looking at it.
