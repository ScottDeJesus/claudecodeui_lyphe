# Plate

A label on a ground plate — the instrument's way of naming what it points at.

- **Provide** the words as `children`, in `canvas-plate` caps: "Reading · Bugfixes", "Runs · 14 / 26". `tone`: `accent` (default) for what the companion is doing now, `quiet` for a helper or a resting label. `leader` (`start` or `end`) draws a hairline toward what it names.
- The plate is `ground` at `opacity-plate`, so it reads over a canvas or a card.
- One short phrase. Plates name; they never instruct.
