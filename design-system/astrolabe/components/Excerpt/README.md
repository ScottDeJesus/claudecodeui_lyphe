# Excerpt

The lines the companion is reading, with its read head moving down the gutter: what is behind it is read, what is ahead is faint.

- **Provide** `path` and `lines` (`{ n, text, kind }`, `kind` `add` or `del` for a diff) — or `text` and `start`. `at` is the line being read; `by` names the companion for its Footprint ("Reading"), `label` changes that word.
- The read head is `accent` and moves on `ease-lock`; the line under it sits on `accent-tint`. Additions and deletions take the `positive` and `danger` soft fills, with + and − in the gutter.
- Show an excerpt only while the companion reads it or while you are looking at it; the whole file is one call away.
- It is text, so it lives in what a click opened, or beside the companion's line while it is reading aloud to you. Never place it on a screen by default.
