# AtWork

The instrument docked beside your work, on a loop. At rest it is small and still live: a run counts up at the foot. A chat needs you: its mark turns accent and the companion says so in one line, and nothing lands on your work. The instrument leans in, the ring turns to that chat, and its question opens from the mark. While the question is open the core flattens to listen. Your word picks an answer and the core writes it to that chat, its tendril aimed at the chat's mark. Then the opening folds back, the mark goes quiet, and the instrument returns to the foot. Reach for it yourself at any time.
