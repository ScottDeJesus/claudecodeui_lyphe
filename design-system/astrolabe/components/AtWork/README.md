# AtWork

The instrument docked beside your work, on a loop. At rest it is small and still live: a run counts up at the foot. A chat needs you: its slot turns accent and the companion says so in one line, and nothing lands on your work. The instrument leans in, the hoop turns to that chat, and its question opens from its slot. While the question is open the core flattens to listen. Your word picks an answer and the core writes it to that chat, its tendril aimed at the chat's slot and a line out to it. Then the opening folds back, the slot goes quiet, and the instrument returns to the foot. Reach for it yourself at any time.
