# Hoop

Sets of things you turn through, as slots on a band that stands round the core, seen from a little above. Each thing has a face you can read and press, and the whole set reads at a glance without a list.

- **Provide** `runs`, in their order round the hoop. Each is `{ id, label, items }`, and an item is `{ id, label, detail, state, progress, agents, weight }`. A run's things stand side by side with its `label` along the ground, and a little air between runs. A Core passed as `children` stands in the middle, and `onCore` is a press on it.
- **What it is doing** is its square. `idle` is lit and still; `asleep` is the frame alone (parked, silent, no live host); `working` turns a ring of `accent` dashes in it; `waiting` fills it `accent`, breathing, because it is owed your word; `done` is `positive-dot`; `failed` is `danger-dot`; `more` is three dots, a run's further things, that opens the rest.
- **How far** is its top edge, lit from its start as far as its `progress`: a checklist done, checks passing, a reply waiting to be read.
- **Who is helping** is a bead at its foot for each of its `agents`, up to three.
- **How much it matters** is how tall it stands: `weight` 1 the band's full height, 0 the shortest. Leave it out and every slot stands full.
- **What faces you is read out under it**, in two lines: the run and the thing's name, then its `detail`. Point at another slot and that one is read instead. Nothing is written on the band itself but the runs' names, and a hoop standing small (`names: false`, `reading: false`) writes none.
- **Mechanics.** Drag it and the slot under your finger stays under it; let go and the nearest slot comes to the front. Tap a slot and it comes round; tap the one at the front and it opens (`onOpen(item, run, point)`). On a touch screen that is two taps: the first shows what is chosen, the second goes to it. The wheel and the arrows step it a slot at a time; Home and End go to the first and last; Enter opens. `onChange(item, run)` fires as another comes to the front. It never turns by itself.
- **While an Opening hangs from it**, pass `hold`: a press on another slot turns it round and opens it at once, so the reader moves from one thing to the next without closing first.
- **What faces you, and where a slot is.** `value` brings a thing to the front (`defaultValue` once). Pass a ref: `pointOf(item)` is the slot's place on screen, for an Opening to hang from; `azimuthOf(item)` is its direction from the core, for the Core's `azimuth`.
- **The core's dealings.** `link: { id, way }` runs a line from the core's edge to that thing's slot, a bead travelling it: `in` while the core reads from it, `out` while it writes to it.
- **One measure worn by the core, or two.** `gauge` is `{ value, label, detail, warn, danger }`: a flat ring round the core, filled clockwise from twelve with a bead at its head. Pass two and the second is worn just outside the first. Each is `ink-muted` until it passes `warn` (0.8), then `warn-dot`, then `danger-dot` from `danger`. Point at one and a plate names it; `onGauge(point, index)` is a press on it, 0 the inner and 1 the outer.
- **Keys.** It is one slider: arrows turn it a slot at a time, Home and End go to the first and last, Enter opens what faces you.
- It fills its container and stands as wide as the room allows; `lean` (0.52) is how far it tips toward you, less for a larger core. Use it for any set you turn through, from a handful of things to a hundred: a run past what it should show ends in a `more` slot. Never label the slots, never put a legend beside it, and never pair it with a list of the same things.
