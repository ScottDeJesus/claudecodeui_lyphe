# Spinner

Something on its way — a read, a load, a turn being prepared: the instrument's thinking loop with its words.

- **Provide** `label` (what is coming, in sentence case with an ellipsis: "Reading the roadmap…") and `size` (44).
- The dashes are `accent` on a `hairline` track and turn once every 2.4 s; under reduced motion they hold still.
- It is a status region, so the label is read out. Replace it with the content the moment it lands; never leave a loop turning over content that is already there.
