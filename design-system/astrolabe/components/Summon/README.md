# Summon

The one call away: drawn out of the core, a line you say or type, and results that arrive as you go.

- **Provide** `open`, `onClose`, `query` and `onQuery`, the matching `items` (`{ id, source, label, hint }`) and `onPick`. `by` names the companion for the mark beside the line; `listening` (with a `signal`) puts a live trace of your voice in the line while you speak.
- It arrives from the core on `ease-lobe` and focuses its line. ↑ and ↓ move, ↵ opens, Esc closes — the keys are shown at its foot.
- Results arrive in order, a beat apart. Show what matches, newest and most likely first; never a list of everything.
- It is how anything absent comes back: give it the history, the settings, every chat, every card. Bind it to the core's key.
