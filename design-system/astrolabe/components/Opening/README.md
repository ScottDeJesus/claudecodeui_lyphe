# Opening

What a deliberate click opened, hung from the mark it came out of. It is the one place where words, lists and history live.

- **Provide** `open`; `anchor`, a point in client coordinates or a function that returns one, such as `() => rings.pointOf(ring, item)`; `onClose`; a `source` label; a `title`; an optional `status` with its `tone`; and the detail as `children`.
- It unfolds on the lock spring, on whichever side has room, `size-opening` wide, joined to its mark by an `accent` hairline that follows the mark. With less than 560px of room it is a sheet at the foot.
- One at a time. Escape, the back arrow at its head, or a press anywhere else folds it back. Focus moves into it when it opens; give it back to the object when it closes. Mark the object's wrapper `data-ast-keeps-opening` so a press on the object can turn to another thing without closing it first.
- **It never opens itself.** An object that needs you comes up to full strength and waits. The opening is yours to make, or the companion's when it is speaking to you about that thing.
- Inside it go the words-and-lists components: Track or Steps, Trace, KeyValueList, Excerpt, Choice, and the controls that act on the thing. It sits on `panel` at `radius-panel` with `shadow-float`.
