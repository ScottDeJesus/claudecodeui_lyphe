# Composer

The line you talk or type through, and the sign of what became of what you sent. It grows with what you type and carries files as chips. It has one button: Send when there is something to send, Stop while work runs and the line is empty.

- **Provide** `value`, `onChange` and `onSend`; `talk` for the talk press, or `onListen` and `listening` (with a `signal`) for dictation; `placeholder` ("Say it or type it"), `sendLabel` ("Send").
- **It grows.** ↵ sends, with or without Ctrl or Cmd; Shift and ↵ is a new line. It grows to `maxRows` (7) lines and then scrolls. At one line it is `size-core-rest` tall so its buttons keep `size-control`.
- **Files ride in it.** Pass `files` (`{ id, name, size, url }`) and they are chips above the text, each with its ×; `onRemoveFile` takes one out. Pass `onFiles` and files can be picked (the plus, quiet beside your words), pasted, or dropped on it. Set `dropping` while a file is dragged over your work and the line takes the `accent` edge. The line never decides what is too big or too many: sort the files yourself and say what was refused in a Notice above it.
- **Its lower edge is the status of your last message.** Pass `busy` (true, or a few words for assistive tech) while it is on its way and the edge crawls `accent` dashes, the same dashes a working span crawls. Pass `progress` 0–1 and the edge fills as the work gets done. Nothing else on screen needs to say "working".
- **One button.** Pass `onStop` while a turn is running: with nothing typed the button is Stop. Type, and it is Send again: set `sendLabel` to "Queue" when what you send will wait for the turn to end. `onHold` fires when Send is held for half a second, and the press that follows sends nothing; hang the choice it opens (Queue or Steer, Send later) from the button in an Opening. Never add a second button for Stop.
- **The talk press.** Pass `talk` (`{ state, onToggle, name, binding, error }`) and the mic stands before Send. One press opens the mic and the next stops it and sends what was heard: `idle` is the quiet mic ("Talk to Kaylee"), `listening` is lit with a breathing edge and your voice traced in the line ("Stop and send"), `transcribing` turns dashes round the mic and is still pressable ("press to talk again"). `binding` ("F8") joins its title. `error` is said above the line in `warn-ink` and the mic takes the same ink, until the next press. The mic never takes the focus from the field.
- **Its buttons are their marks alone**: the plus, the mic, Stop and Send carry no word, only a title and a name for assistive tech. Queue is the one that needs its word, and it drops it too when the line itself is narrower than 480px, as on a phone.
- Its border turns `accent` while listening, and an `accent` hairline draws under it on focus.
- It belongs in the Scene's `controls`, at the foot, or inside a Summon. Never more than one on screen.
