# YourWords

Your exact line, quoted — the anchor of anything that moved on your word.

- **Provide** the line as `children`, exactly as you said or typed it: never a paraphrase, never the companion's summary. `via` (`spoken` or `typed`), `at`.
- It is set in `said`, the only place that style appears; the caption is `caption-caps` in `ink-muted`: "YOU · SPOKEN · 09:31".
- Show it wherever your words are the reason something happened: an action locked, a ruling the companion keeps, a correction.
- The quote marks hang outside the line. No border, no fill: your words need no box.
