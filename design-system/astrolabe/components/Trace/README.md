# Trace

What happened, newest first: the history that stays one call away instead of on screen.

- **Provide** `events`: `{ id, at, source, text }`, plus `status` and `tone` for a state, `by` and `did` when the companion did it (a Footprint), or `words` and `via` when it moved on your word (YourWords). `max` (12).
- Show it inside a Sheet or a Summon result — never as a permanent column.
- The newest event's bead is `accent` and lands on the lobe; older beads are hollow. New events arrive at the top, on `ease-lock`.
- It is a list of words, so it lives in what a click opened: an Opening or a Sheet. Never place it on a screen by default. When history has to be seen at a glance, it is a Track whose steps are the events, and this is what a step opens.
